        Help
        
    
          
  • iPhone: Safari can't use Bluetooth printers. Open this page in the free Bluefy browser instead, and bookmark it there.
    • 
          
  • Printer not found: make sure it's on (green light), close RLabel, and tap “Show all Bluetooth devices”. It may appear as “RP425…”.
    • 
          
  • Copy & paste: copy a label (Share → Copy, or press and hold an image → Copy), then tap “Paste a copied label”. If nothing happens, press and hold the paste box that appears and tap Paste.
    • 
          
  • Shipping labels: Letter-size PDFs work; the app finds the label on the page. To change it, tap Adjust (or the preview): drag to move, pinch to zoom, Rotate, or switch to Freeform and drag the corners.
    • 
          
  • Says “Sent” but nothing prints: switch the printer off and on (a half-received label can leave it waiting), then print again. If big labels still don't print, lower the sending speed limit under Advanced.
    • 
          
  • Label position drifts: tap “Learn label size”, or hold the printer's feed button until it beeps.
    • 
          
  • Faint print: raise Print darkness. Upside down: tick “Flip them”.
